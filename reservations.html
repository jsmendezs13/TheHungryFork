About the restaurant

The Hungry Fork is a steakhouse on Stone Street, in the old cobblestone heart of Lower Manhattan. We cook steak the way it should be: the T-bone, the sliced steak, and a salmon with sweet corn, served in a warm, dark dining room made for a long dinner. Open every day, from noon to 11 pm.
