What this service is

The Hungry Fork shows restaurant menus and collects ratings and comments from people who have eaten the dishes — we call them tasters. Fork’s Rating is the score those tasters produce. We are not paid by restaurants to raise a score, and restaurants cannot remove a review they dislike.

Checking in at a restaurant

A check-in is a record that you were at a restaurant on a particular day. You tap Check In, your phone shows a code for ninety seconds, and a member of restaurant staff confirms it. It is optional, and nothing else on this site depends on it.

  • One check-in per restaurant per day. Showing the code again the same day adds nothing.
  • Only a member of that restaurant’s staff can confirm a visit, and never their own.
  • Check in only when you are really there. Do not ask staff to confirm a visit that did not happen, and do not lend your code to somebody else — it would record the visit against your account anyway.
  • We can remove check-ins we have good reason to believe are not real, and suspend an account that does this repeatedly.

A check-in is a record, not a promise. It does not by itself entitle you to anything. Where a restaurant chooses to offer something for returning customers, that offer is the restaurant’s — see Promotions below.

How moderation works, and who can do what

We think you should know exactly where the power sits, because it is what makes a rating worth reading:

  • A restaurant can report a review to us. It cannot hide it, edit it, or delete it — not a word of it, not its score.
  • Only Seb’s Analytics LLC can remove a review, and only for breaking the rules above. A bad score is not a reason.
  • If we remove your review, we will tell you why in a message to your account.
  • We can suspend an account that repeatedly breaks these rules.

Always speak to restaurant staff directly before you order. Seb’s Analytics LLC does not prepare the food and is not responsible for what a kitchen serves you.
